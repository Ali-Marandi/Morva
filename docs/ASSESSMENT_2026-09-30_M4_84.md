## M4.84 — Historical integrity primitive adoption guard

M4.84 adds a structural regression contract over the M4.77/M4.78/M4.80 historical-integrity runtimes.

### Implemented
- AST-based validation requires the three runtimes to import canonical_sha256 from the shared primitive.
- The guard rejects reintroduction of local hashlib.sha256 imports/calls.
- The guard rejects local json.dumps fingerprint serialization in those runtimes.
- A dedicated CI gate runs Ruff and focused pytest coverage.
