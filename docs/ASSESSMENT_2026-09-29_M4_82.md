# Morva Technical Assessment — 2026-09-29 — M4.82

## M4.82 independent M4.81 verification

M4.82 adds an independent verification layer immediately after M4.81 persistence.

### Implemented
- Independently validates persisted M4.81 verification receipts.
- Reconstructs M4.80 from the M4.79 receipt, M4.77 snapshot and point-in-time M4.76 result history.
- Emits deterministic identity, fingerprint and validity mismatch blockers.
- Adds a read-only verification endpoint and focused tamper/contract coverage.
- Adds a dedicated CI gate for Ruff, pytest and migration validation.

### Boundary
M4.82 remains verification/readiness metadata only. No provider execution, credentials, payment mutation, legal-rate inference, or production authorization is introduced.

### External prerequisites
Authoritative legal artifacts, approved population-specific rules and matrices, authoritative organization/personnel master data, certified historical replay corpus, production infrastructure controls, DR evidence, official provider contracts and independent operational/legal/finance/security certification remain external prerequisites.
