# M4.11 — Load Validation Evidence

M4.11 turns target-scale test output into a structured evidence contract.

The record binds the workload profile, employee-population scope, target/processed population, elapsed time, throughput and deterministic result fingerprint to accepted authoritative `load_validation` evidence.

The contract requires a passing 10,000+ employee validation, distinct reviewer/approver provenance and current authority evidence.

This is engineering evidence only. It does not establish production capacity, SLO compliance or operational certification.
